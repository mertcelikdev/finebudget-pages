Support

For help, contact the developer using the email link below. Include your app version, device model, operating system and a short description of the issue. Do not include salary, balances, notes, bank information, card details or screenshots containing financial information.

For purchases, use Restore purchases on the Pro screen with the original Apple or Google Play account. Manage or cancel a subscription through the store settings. If a payment is pending, access updates after the store confirms it.

If an earned reward cannot be saved, keep the app open and use Retry saving your reward. You will not be asked to watch another ad for that pending reward. If no ad is available, all core budgeting features remain usable.

Language and ad privacy preferences are available in Settings. Core budgeting remains usable without internet access.
