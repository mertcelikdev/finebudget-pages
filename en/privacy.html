Privacy policy

This policy describes Paycheck Budget. The developer and contact address are listed below.

Your income, expenses, bills, savings, accounts, categories and notes are stored in a local database on your device. Core budgeting works offline. The app does not connect to your bank or synchronize budget records between devices. Changing language does not change your currency or user-written data.

When the optional account feature is available, Supabase processes your email address, account identifier and technical information needed for sign-in. Google processes information for Google sign-in. Your account identifier is shared with RevenueCat to associate your subscription. Account availability depends on the app version; budget records are not synced through the account.

If you choose to export and share a password-protected backup, the file is sent to the app or service you select, which may store it on another device or in the cloud. Your selected service's privacy and retention policies apply. Keep the password and backup safe; deleting app data does not delete copies you exported.

Purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store and RevenueCat. These services process purchase receipts, product and entitlement information, and an app user identifier to validate subscriptions and restore purchases. Payment details are entered directly with the store. The app does not send budget amounts, notes or bank details to RevenueCat.

Google AdMob provides small banner ads on Transactions and Reports and optional rewarded ads. Ad services may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions and diagnostics. We request non-personalized ads; this does not mean the ad services process no data. The app does not supply financial values or notes for ad targeting. Consent and required privacy choices are managed through Google's User Messaging Platform. Where available, revisit Ad privacy preferences in Settings → Privacy and support.

A rewarded ad can enable Pro features for 24 hours. The expiry is stored locally on this device and does not start a paid subscription. Local rewards do not transfer between devices. Subscription entitlement caching is managed by RevenueCat.

If crash reporting is enabled in the release, Sentry processes sanitized technical crash and device information. Our app strips financial values and user-written text from reports and does not attach budget records. Anonymous analytics events are currently handled locally without an external analytics sink.

Notifications are optional and scheduled locally. Reminder text does not contain amounts or bill names. Widgets can display your Safe to Spend amount on your home screen if you turn off the default privacy setting. Anyone who can view that screen may see a visible amount.

Reset budget data in Settings to remove budget records, or uninstall the app to remove local app storage. Resetting the budget does not cancel a subscription. Manage subscriptions in your store account. Stores and service providers retain their own transaction and technical records according to their policies and applicable requirements.

Budget records remain on your device until you delete them, reset the budget, or remove the app's local storage. You control any exported backups separately. To ask about access, correction or deletion of information processed by service providers, contact us using the address below; we will handle requests subject to applicable law and provider requirements. Where available, change or withdraw ad privacy choices in the app's privacy center and use your device's privacy controls.

If you contact support, we receive your email address and the contents of your message to respond and manage your request. Please do not send financial records, passwords or payment details. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle requests and meet applicable obligations; contact us to request deletion, subject to those obligations.

This product is intended for adult personal budgeting. We do not design ads or features for children. Contact the developer below for privacy questions or requests relating to data processed by our service providers. Do not send financial records or payment details with a support request.

Provider policies: policies.google.com/privacy; revenuecat.com/privacy; apple.com/legal/privacy; sentry.io/privacy. We update this policy when app behavior changes.
